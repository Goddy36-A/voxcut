# Who Ideawood Studio is for

One offline-friendly Windows studio for people who **teach, preach, broadcast, train and sell** - and need videos
without a team, a subscription, or lots of data. The header's **"I work as"** menu only sets friendly defaults
(`voxcut/workspaces.py`); every tool stays available.

| Workspace | Typical job | Use these tools | Default look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tutor / Lecturer | lessons, revision, online classes | Lecture recorder (no time limit, whiteboard, Board AI), Subtitles | plain captions |
| Church / Ministry | sermons, verses, testimonies | Subtitles (word highlight), Create video (verse/invitation), voice cleanup | large highlighted captions |
| Radio / Podcast | turn audio into watchable clips | Subtitles, voice cleanup, Create video (teasers) | big middle captions, highlight |
| Business / Marketing | promos, product, customer stories | Create video (AI), Subtitles | highlighted captions |
| Training / HR / NGO | onboarding, policy, impact updates | Lecture recorder, Subtitles, templates | plain captions |
| Software demos | tutorials, release notes | Lecture recorder (any window, cursor ring, chapters), Subtitles | plain captions |
| Interviews / Journalism | transcripts and clips | Subtitles (TXT transcript + SRT) | plain captions |
| Coach / Consultant | workshops and session recaps | Lecture recorder, Subtitles, templates | plain captions |

## Why these groups (assumptions to test, not research)
* They record **long, talking-heavy content** that benefits from captions (noisy rooms, muted phones, second-language viewers).
* Many cannot pay recurring fees in dollars or stream/upload gigabytes: recording, cleanup and captions run **on the PC**;
  only short text goes online for the optional AI features.
* Trust and reach matter more than polish: consistent intros/outros, clear audio, readable captions.

## Honest gaps
Windows only; no phone app; ASR accuracy on local accents is unmeasured; Luganda is unsupported by Whisper; stock visuals in
Create video are generic; no cloud sync/collaboration; no teams/admin features yet.

## Next steps that would help most
1. Validate with 5 people per profession (one real recording each) and note what they edit most in the caption table.
2. Auto-subtitle straight after a recording; chapter list from the transcript for YouTube.
3. Per-profession intro/outro and lower-third presets (name + title) for sermons, lectures and promos.
